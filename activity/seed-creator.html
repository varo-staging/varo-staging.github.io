Program Rules

Keep It Simple. Keep It Fair.

Clear rules designed to protect the creator community.

  • The first 30 creators who pass X and Discord verification become Seed Creators and receive a $20 Bonus.
  • Each Seed Creator may invite multiple friends, but can earn only one invite reward.
  • The invitee must make a single top-up of at least $10 within 3 days of signup.
  • Once qualified, both the invitee and inviter receive a $10 Bonus.
  • Bonuses are non-withdrawable, non-refundable, and valid for 14 days or until the campaign ends, whichever comes first.
